Common questions

What is Time to Move?

Time to Move, also called Move10, 活动一下 and ちょっと動こう, is an iPhone app for scheduled stand-up and work-break reminders. Its App Store ID is 6805086796.

Does it automatically open over other apps?

No. It uses iOS local notifications. Notification delivery and sound depend on your iPhone notification settings and Focus mode.

Which languages are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German and Spanish.

Do I need to start a timer every day?

No. Once your schedule is set, reminders are scheduled automatically for your selected workdays.

Is this a Pomodoro timer or workout app?

No. It is a focused reminder for standing up during desk work. It does not provide exercise courses or medical advice.

Does the app require an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your settings and records remain on your device.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app has a 1-day trial followed by an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit. Check the App Store purchase sheet for your local price.
